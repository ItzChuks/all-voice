About the show

Stories, in their own voice.

Some stories are meant to be heard. Our podcast carries the voices of the people we work with across Nova Scotia, told in their own words and at their own pace. We are beginning with This Is My Story, a series created with seniors at Northwood Care, and the collection will grow as new voices join.

Listen here on this page, or follow the show on Spotify, Apple Podcasts, or wherever you find your podcasts.
