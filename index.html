01  /  About Us

A registered Nova Scotia non‑profit, based in Halifax.

We believe that when people are given the tools and the space to tell their own stories, communities grow closer, understanding deepens, and change follows. We run storytelling workshops, media production labs, and community screenings with seniors, youth, newcomers, and racialised communities across the province.
