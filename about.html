About Us

A registered Nova Scotia non‑profit, based in Halifax.

Unity, diversity, and social inclusion through media and storytelling.

01  /  Our story

What we believe.

We believe that when people are given the tools and the space to tell their own stories, communities grow closer, understanding deepens, and change follows.

We run storytelling workshops, media production labs, and community screenings with seniors, youth, newcomers, and racialised communities across the province. Participants learn to write, film, and edit. What they produce is theirs, and it is shared on their terms.

02  /  What guides us

Our values

Ubuntu

We embrace the philosophy that I am because we are, and we work from a belief in our interconnectedness.

Inclusivity

We welcome diversity and create spaces where all voices are heard and respected.

Empowerment

We help people find their voice, tell their stories, and create positive change.

Respect

We treat everyone with dignity, building a culture of understanding and acceptance.

Collaboration

We work alongside partners, volunteers, and community members to achieve our mission.

Integrity

We hold to high ethical standards, transparency, and accountability in everything we do.

Land Acknowledgement

All Voices Society acknowledges that we live and work in Mi'kma'ki, the ancestral and unceded territory of the Mi'kmaq People, covered by the Treaties of Peace and Friendship first signed in 1725. We also acknowledge the enduring presence and contributions of African Nova Scotians, whose ancestors have lived here for more than four hundred years across fifty-two historic communities.
